Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What SnapNotes stores

SnapNotes (the Chrome extension) saves the notes you write, the timestamps they're attached to, the titles/thumbnails/links of videos you've taken notes on, the room names you create, and your free-trial/license status. All of this is stored using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) directly on your own device.

What SnapNotes does not do

  • The extension does not run its own servers and does not transmit your notes, video history, or any other saved data anywhere. Nothing is sent to us, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • The extension does not use analytics or tracking scripts.
  • The extension does not read or collect anything from any site other than YouTube video ("watch") pages, and only what's needed to show the current timestamp, video title, and thumbnail.

Payments

If you choose to unlock unlimited notes, you'll be taken from the extension to this website, where payment is processed entirely by Paddle.com, our payment provider and merchant of record. We do not see or store your card details — Paddle handles that, subject to Paddle's own privacy policy. After a successful payment, this page generates a license code in your browser and displays it to you; that code is not transmitted to us either — it's simply a formatted code you copy into the extension yourself.

Chrome sync

SnapNotes intentionally uses chrome.storage.local rather than chrome.storage.sync, so your notes stay on the device you created them on and are not automatically synced by Chrome across your devices or through your Google account.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data (notes, rooms, and license status) along with it, per Chrome's standard behavior for extension storage.
