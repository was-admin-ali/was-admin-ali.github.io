The product

SnapNotes is a Chrome extension for taking timestamped notes while watching YouTube videos. A free plan includes 10 saved notes, total. The paid "Lifetime" plan is a single one-time payment that removes that limit, permanently, for that license — it is not a subscription and does not renew or charge again.

Paddle's own buyer terms apply to the transaction alongside these terms.

License delivery

After payment completes, a license code is generated and displayed on this site for you to copy into the extension. If you don't see it, or lose it later, contact us at the email below with your Paddle receipt and we'll help you recover it.
